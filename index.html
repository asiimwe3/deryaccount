DeryAccount — Privacy Policy

Derycode · Offline Accounting & POS · Effective 1 September 2026 · Version 1.0

Offline-first by design

Your data stays on your phone

DeryAccount is a local, offline application. All your business records — sales, stock, customers, expenses and books of account — are stored in the app's private database on your device only. DeryAccount does not send your accounting data to any server, and there is no Derycode cloud holding your books.

What we never do

We do not sell, share, or monetise your data. We do not collect your customers' data. We do not track your sales. There are no analytics and no advertising identifiers inside the accounting functions.

Crash reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, a technical crash report (error details, app version, and device model — nothing else) may be sent to Derycode's support system so we can fix bugs faster. Crash reports never contain your business records, customer names, amounts, or any accounting data. You can prevent crash reports at any time by using the app offline.

Permissions the app uses

Storage: to save receipts, reports and books as files in the visible DeryAccount folder, and to attach backups when YOU choose to share them. Camera: only for scanning product barcodes. Bluetooth: only for connecting your receipt printer. Install packages: only for installing app updates you approve. Phone state (optional): not required — every accounting feature works offline.

When you share

If you tap Share (backup, receipt, invoice), Android opens the app you choose (WhatsApp, Gmail, Drive, TeraBox). What happens in those apps is governed by their own policies — DeryAccount only attaches the file you selected.

Subscription payments

If you pay for a plan, the payment is processed on our website by the mobile-money provider (PesaPal). DeryAccount stores only the activation status on your device. We never see or store your PIN, and card/mobile-money details are handled entirely by the payment provider under their own privacy policy.

App updates

On each launch the app checks our public GitHub release page for a newer version. This check sends no personal data. Updates are always installed only after you approve them.

Children

DeryAccount is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
